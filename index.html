FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

The questions we hear most.

Is this GoNetspeed's official website?

No. This site is operated by an independent authorized dealer for GoNetspeed. We are not GoNetspeed and we are not an internet service provider. GoNetspeed's own website is gonetspeed.com. We can place new orders on GoNetspeed's behalf; the service itself is provided, installed, billed and supported by GoNetspeed.

Does it cost more to order through you?

No. You get the same GoNetspeed plans, rates and promotions available at your address. We're compensated by the provider when we set up a new customer, which never changes what you pay.

Why don't you list a flat monthly price for every speed?

Because GoNetspeed genuinely prices by location — rates differ by state and by address, and the promotional structure changes. The 3 Gig plan has published national promo pricing ($34.99/mo for months 1–3, $69.99/mo for months 4–12), so we show it. For the other speeds we'd rather quote you the real number for your home on the call than publish one that turns out to be wrong.

What happens after the promotional period?

Then-current retail rates apply. We'll tell you what that rate is for your address before you order — ask the agent to walk you through months 1–3, months 4–12 and the ongoing rate so there are no surprises.

How do I know if GoNetspeed reaches my house?

Fiber is built street by street, so availability is address-specific — a neighbor two blocks away may have it when you don't, and vice versa. Call us with your service address and we'll check it directly.

What if GoNetspeed isn't available where I live?

We'll tell you that plainly. Because we're also an authorized retailer for Frontier, Kinetic by Windstream and EarthLink, we can check those on the same call if you'd like. There's no obligation, and we won't move you off GoNetspeed if GoNetspeed is available to you.

I'm already a GoNetspeed customer and need help with my bill or connection.

We can't help with existing accounts — we only place new orders. Please contact GoNetspeed directly at (855) 891-7291 or through gonetspeed.com for billing, technical support, moves or cancellations.

Is there a contract or a data cap?

GoNetspeed residential fiber plans are advertised with no annual contract and no data caps. Specific terms are set by GoNetspeed and confirmed at the time of order.
