FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is this Frontier's website?

No. We are an independent Frontier authorized sales agent. We are not Frontier Communications and we are not an internet service provider. We can check availability and place new Frontier orders; the service itself is provided, installed, billed and supported by Frontier.

Does it cost more to order through you?

No. You get the same Frontier plans and promotions available at your address. We're paid by the provider when we set up a new customer, which doesn't change what you pay.

Why don't you list prices?

Frontier's pricing and promotions depend on your address and change over time. We'd rather give you the real monthly price for your home on the call, including what happens after any promotional period, than publish a number that turns out to be wrong.

Can I get the offers I saw on frontier.com?

Some Frontier promotions, such as certain reward cards, are only for orders placed online at frontier.com. When you call, we'll tell you exactly which offers apply to an order placed with us before you decide, so there are no surprises.

What if Frontier Fiber isn't available at my address?

We'll tell you plainly. This line only places Frontier orders, so if Frontier Fiber doesn't reach your street yet, we'll let you know rather than try to sell you something else.

I'm already a Frontier customer and need help with my bill or service.

We can't help with existing accounts — we only set up new service. Please contact Frontier directly through frontier.com for billing, technical support, moves or cancellations.

What do I need when I call?

Just your service address and a rough idea of how your household uses the internet. Most calls take about 10 minutes.
